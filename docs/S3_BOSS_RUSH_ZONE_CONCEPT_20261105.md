# Sonic Chaos — S3 Boss Rush Zone: Concept and Feasibility Assessment
**5 November 2026 · Chief-approved S3 architecture · Manager-san #10 · Reuse existing boss arenas unchanged, build only connecting sections**

## Chief's design preference
Rather than a plain boss select or menu-to-menu fight sequence, Chief described the SMS1 boss mode as **short chained stages with title cards/transitions**, three lives and available rings. Chief now **explicitly clarified**: **REUSE THE SIX EXISTING BOSS ARENAS AS-IS**, preserving their original **camera rules, transitions, spawn coordinates, collision geometry, triggers, boss routines, environment mechanics and timing**; visually recolour arena/background assets into an Electric Egg-inspired palette **without reconstructing the arenas**. New level-design work should concentrate on **short interstitial travel segments**, which themselves may be lifted/rearranged from the existing corresponding zones, plus the enclosing six-fight progression. Ring supply is limited, possibly **none newly offered between encounters 5 and 6**. This is **one cohesive custom Boss Rush Zone in player experience**, not a mandatory single GameMaker room nor six redesigned boss fights. Manager judges this significantly less risky than rebuilding every arena; source room-identity constraints still need a careful adapter/fallback proof.

## Proposed player experience (not yet frozen product details)

**Chief's concrete MGHZ connector example (5 Nov):** To join existing structures, **reuse chunks of their original platforms**, remove the MGHZ lower safety/escape area beneath the special platform if it creates unnecessary verticality, and introduce **visible death-pit borders** to prevent walking beyond a deliberately locked camera. Keep transitions *short and playable*, with **small ring supplies** between early bosses. This qualifies the earlier absolute “unchanged arena” language: **preserve the required active boss combat core and its camera/trigger/spike/reveal mechanics**, but **permit documented challenge-only pruning of surplus copied terrain** and the addition of safe boundary pits. The accepted Classic/S2 original rooms stay byte-identical; test Sonic/Tails jump, Tails flight, knockback and boss entry/exit against any new pit. A trimmed optional safety route is a deliberate challenge difficulty change, not full original map-collision parity. [Binding sprint update](S3_BOSS_RUSH_ZONE_SPRINT_20261105.md).


- A single named **Boss Rush Zone** with a consistent Electric Egg-inspired visual identity, 6 compact sequential sections. Each stage has a short themed approach segment, **the corresponding **boss fight core preserved mechanically** in its original world-space/camera contract, with optional surrounding/lower safety terrain trimmed only in challenge copies**, then a transition/title card into the next. **Use 6 distinct internal rooms/segments under one cohesive zone identity if the engine requires them**; no requirement to weld all bosses into one physical GameMaker room. Start from whole original arena data, not an approximation of its shape. Limited Chief-requested trimming of surplus lower/safety terrain and addition of camera-boundary death pits is permitted if required boss mechanics and fair playable arcs remain intact.
- Sequence: THZ3, GPZ3, SEZ3, MGHZ3, AQZ3, EEZ3. Keep original attack patterns, boss hitboxes, health, hazard interactions, iconic spike/tree trap, arena geometry and camera reveal logic **identical to accepted S2 under equivalent boss-state/room-coordinate setups**, not merely approximately equivalent. Trim or bypass lengthy normal level approaches, **not the boss arena or its trigger corridor**. New short connector terrain should be assembled largely from authentic existing chunks. Test every preserved arena at its original reference and Wide coordinate contracts.
- Start with **3 lives**, limited/placed ring supplies, **no continues and no checkpoints as initial balance suggestion**, with run-ending death/restart policy to be proven pleasant. **No new ring allocation between bosses 5 and 6** is Chief's challenge preference; remaining carried rings might persist into final encounter depending on canonical transition feasibility. Keep available pickups <100 total if avoiding accidental original 100-ring extra-life/SS triggers; do not rewrite native ring-award mechanics simply to mask problem.
- Character selection: both Sonic and Tails with their normal native moves. A clear discoverable Enhanced-only challenge entry outside the five save slots (exact title/Data Select UI placement to be designed). No Classic entry, no added toggle in Enhanced display Options. A challenge run **does not create/write S2 save data**, change Emeralds, advance zone frontier, grant ordinary endgame completion or overwrite any slot. Optional separate challenge completion flag/record is a distinct future design choice, not granted in current spec.
- Score/time/challenge results can use original-themed title cards, but don't assume ordinary Act3 results or EEZ ending directly suit new mode. Use a distinct isolated challenge-flow owner and short intentional transitions, preserving the original gameplay and Z80/PSG sound owner.

## Technical feasibility facts (Manager checked accepted E0-R1 source; S2 later GML is private/local)
- Six original boss GML programmes exist: `SCR_chaos_boss` (THZ), `SCR_chaos_gpz_boss`, `SCR_chaos_sez_boss`, `SCR_chaos_mghz_boss`, `SCR_chaos_aqz_boss`, `SCR_chaos_eez_boss`. The original 18-act table contains their six Act3 rooms. **Existing bosses are zone/room-contextual**, not free-floating reusable objects; `SCR_chaos_level` dispatches via `chaos_is_*()` based on hard-coded rooms and installs per-zone layouts, collision, object pools and enemy/boss registers.
- GPZ, SEZ, MGHZ, AQZ and EEZ original boss scripts contain absolute/map-derived camera positions, height/width conditions and callbacks; a new run requires carefully constructed original-equivalent arena factories and coordinate transformations, not simply moving original boss sprites to EEZ room. For MGHZ3 in particular preserve the Chief-accepted spike-gated tree reveal/lock semantics; avoid recreating the original late boss reveal defect.
- **Visual EEZ palette** applies to arena/connector environment **presentation only** while their original underlying tile identity, terrain flags, collision, camera and boss scripting remain untouched. Construct a scoped Enhanced-only palette/tileset presentation adapter, not a blanket game-wide shader or destructive replacement of shared source assets. **Do not recolour Sonic/Tails or boss sprites globally.** AQZ water boundaries, MGHZ spikes, GPZ projectiles, SEZ hazards and the Electric Egg final sequence must remain readable and mechanically identical; where an Electric Egg palette assignment renders a mechanic ambiguous or cannot be separated from collision, preserve its original functional effect palette while keeping surrounding terrain thematically unified.
- **EEZ final fight has conditional phase:** in `SCR_chaos_eez_boss`, Sonic with the first five Emerald bits (`collected==31`) follows the full final sequence/gets bit 32, while other cases use a shorter ending route. A Boss Rush run must explicitly define whether final full route is always offered; if used, set only **temporary challenge-local** eligibility and prevent any persistent S2 Emerald/finished-save award or ordinary ending overwrite. This is an important distinct design choice, not an assumed trivial reuse.
- **Main risk:** preserving all original boss lifecycles, terrain, camera/viewport lock/reveal, deaths, Pause and per-zone shutdown/next-section transitions at both 256 reference and 348 Wide. Significant but bounded one-sprint engineering. Do not refactor original Classic boss implementations globally or touch frozen tag/archive. Small protected GML successor-branch guarded hooks may be legitimate if Classic/nonchallenge states demonstrably identical.

## Proposed implementation sequence within a single substantial sprint
1. **Extract/clone the six accepted boss arena data regions, including the original triggering approach, without coordinate translation or rebuilding the fight terrain.** Map original source-level entry/exit, camera/terrain/palette dependencies; perform controlled state/draw/collision parity against accepted S2. Validate MGHZ3 spike-gated reveal, AQZ water and EEZ3 full/partial endings early. If original room identity prevents direct reuse, add the narrowest reversible challenge-only registration/dispatch bridge **without editing accepted source rooms**. This is an internal viability gate, not a new Chief-facing micro-build.
2. Create one challenge-flow controller and six compact internal sections based on **unaltered copies/references to the six accepted arena layouts** (including their original world coords if needed). Assemble only **interstitial approaches/connector passages** from existing original-zone chunks; join sections with original-style title cards/fades and room handoffs. Apply reversible EEZ-themed environment palette presentation per challenge room. Do not hand-rebuild fight cores or move boss placement/trigger positions; allow documented surrounding-area trim/death-pit edits that do not disturb required fight behavior.
3. Implement proposed three-life start, bounded rings, original stage-to-stage transitions and separate game-over/challenge-complete behavior; no new rings between bosses 5/6 unless Chief changes challenge design. Preserve original ring mechanic unless explicitly authorized. Keep Save/Title/Classic/Enhanced state isolated; challenge deaths never write campaign saves, Emerald flags or regular ending records. Exact ring carry and final EEZ phase entitlement need explicit design decisions before implementation; do not infer from original persistent campaign slot.
4. Combined native test of all six encounters for both characters, verifying **boss/camera/terrain/collision and audio parity in cloned encounter regions** against accepted S2; transitions, ring accounting, stage continuity, no softlocks, safe Pause/results/death/CRT presenter, S2 five-slot save bytes unchanged and intact Classic. Compare encounter screenshots in original palette versus EEZ palette with consistent game state (intentional colour change only). One final private Windows build plus full source, visual comparisons, H: recovery, SHA receipt and one Chief play check. No full original campaigns.

## Decisions to settle as part of sprint design (not blockers to conceptual feasibility)
- **How is Boss Rush entered?** Suggest a clear separate Enhanced challenge choice near Data Select or Enhanced title, not replacing five existing slots and not requiring a new save. Prefer an obvious selection rather than a hidden developer key.
- **Final boss route:** show Sonic full five-Emerald EEZ final phase in challenge by default, or respect fresh/zero Emerald route? Recommend deliberate full encounter for challenge value, with no persistence. Tails route unchanged unless Chief deliberately approves an alternate final phase.
- **Challenge record/reward:** visible clear/time score, replay, unlock policy and choice of ring count. Recommend minimal nonpersistent results first, and add per-character best-time records only if they can be isolated safely. This is not S2 campaign completion.
