# Sonic Chaos — S3 Boss Rush Zone: Concept and Feasibility Assessment
**5 November 2026 · Chief's preferred S3 direction · Manager-san #10 · CONCEPT RECORDED, implementation design not yet locked**

## Chief's design preference
Rather than a plain boss select or menu-to-menu fight sequence, Chief described the SMS1 boss mode as **short chained stages with title cards/transitions**, three lives and available rings. Chief prefers to extend this into **one custom Boss Rush Zone**: compact sections and inter-boss playable traversal, original six boss arenas **reconstructed in an Electric Egg-inspired palette**, reused camera transition/lock machinery, very limited ring supplies (potentially **no new rings between bosses 5 and 6**). This is the S3 concept to research; do not automatically build a simple standalone six-boss menu mode. The question was explicitly whether this is too ambitious; Manager judges it **feasible as one substantial, but nontrivial, sprint** if boss callbacks, terrain and camera adaptation are isolated and the original gameplay core is protected.

## Proposed player experience (not yet frozen product details)
- A single named **Boss Rush Zone** with a consistent Electric Egg-inspired visual identity, 6 compact sequential sections. Each stage has a short themed approach segment, canonical boss encounter and a transition/title card into the next. **Use 6 distinct short rooms/segments under one cohesive zone identity where the engine requires them;** no requirement to weld all bosses into one giant physical GameMaker room.
- Sequence: THZ3, GPZ3, SEZ3, MGHZ3, AQZ3, EEZ3. Keep original attack patterns, boss hitboxes, health, hazard interactions, iconic spike/tree trap, arena geography and camera reveal logic **as close to their existing authentic implementations as possible**. Short approach terrain can be custom; don't accidentally alter fairness by recreating arenas inaccurately.
- Start with **3 lives**, limited/placed ring supplies, **no continues and no checkpoints as initial balance suggestion**, with run-ending death/restart policy to be proven pleasant. **No new ring allocation between bosses 5 and 6** is Chief's challenge preference; remaining carried rings might persist into final encounter depending on canonical transition feasibility. Keep available pickups <100 total if avoiding accidental original 100-ring extra-life/SS triggers; do not rewrite native ring-award mechanics simply to mask problem.
- Character selection: both Sonic and Tails with their normal native moves. A clear discoverable Enhanced-only challenge entry outside the five save slots (exact title/Data Select UI placement to be designed). No Classic entry, no added toggle in Enhanced display Options. A challenge run **does not create/write S2 save data**, change Emeralds, advance zone frontier, grant ordinary endgame completion or overwrite any slot. Optional separate challenge completion flag/record is a distinct future design choice, not granted in current spec.
- Score/time/challenge results can use original-themed title cards, but don't assume ordinary Act3 results or EEZ ending directly suit new mode. Use a distinct isolated challenge-flow owner and short intentional transitions, preserving the original gameplay and Z80/PSG sound owner.

## Technical feasibility facts (Manager checked accepted E0-R1 source; S2 later GML is private/local)
- Six original boss GML programmes exist: `SCR_chaos_boss` (THZ), `SCR_chaos_gpz_boss`, `SCR_chaos_sez_boss`, `SCR_chaos_mghz_boss`, `SCR_chaos_aqz_boss`, `SCR_chaos_eez_boss`. The original 18-act table contains their six Act3 rooms. **Existing bosses are zone/room-contextual**, not free-floating reusable objects; `SCR_chaos_level` dispatches via `chaos_is_*()` based on hard-coded rooms and installs per-zone layouts, collision, object pools and enemy/boss registers.
- GPZ, SEZ, MGHZ, AQZ and EEZ original boss scripts contain absolute/map-derived camera positions, height/width conditions and callbacks; a new run requires carefully constructed original-equivalent arena factories and coordinate transformations, not simply moving original boss sprites to EEZ room. For MGHZ3 in particular preserve the Chief-accepted spike-gated tree reveal/lock semantics; avoid recreating the original late boss reveal defect.
- **Visual EEZ palette** should apply principally to new environment terrain/background/approach art and compatible arena tiles (with colour/readability adjustments), **not blanket-recolour Sonic, Tails, original boss sprites or effect logic**. Some fight-specific geometry and water/palette effects are gameplay-significant: preserve mechanics and visible collision/hazard identity, especially AQZ water and MGHZ spikes.
- **EEZ final fight has conditional phase:** in `SCR_chaos_eez_boss`, Sonic with the first five Emerald bits (`collected==31`) follows the full final sequence/gets bit 32, while other cases use a shorter ending route. A Boss Rush run must explicitly define whether final full route is always offered; if used, set only **temporary challenge-local** eligibility and prevent any persistent S2 Emerald/finished-save award or ordinary ending overwrite. This is an important distinct design choice, not an assumed trivial reuse.
- **Main risk:** preserving all original boss lifecycles, terrain, camera/viewport lock/reveal, deaths, Pause and per-zone shutdown/next-section transitions at both 256 reference and 348 Wide. Significant but bounded one-sprint engineering. Do not refactor original Classic boss implementations globally or touch frozen tag/archive. Small protected GML successor-branch guarded hooks may be legitimate if Classic/nonchallenge states demonstrably identical.

## Proposed implementation sequence within a single substantial sprint
1. Map source-level entry/exit conditions and arena coordinate/terrain dependencies across six Act3 bosses. Validate hardest integration examples early: MGHZ3 reveal, AQZ3 water and EEZ3 full/partial ending. **An internal viability gate, not a new Chief-facing micro-build**.
2. Create one custom challenge controller and six compact custom room/section configurations, reuse original boss scripts/resources with isolated adapters. Add coherent EEZ-themed environmental palette/layout and original-style short zone cards/approach corridors.
3. Implement lives/rings balance, short transitions and game-over/challenge-complete behavior. Keep Save/Title/Classic/Enhanced state completely isolated. Distinguish challenge deaths from original endgame and disable unwanted S2 progression hooks in challenge context.
4. Combined native test of all six encounters both characters, contact/presentation/audio/camera parity in identical reference arenas, continuity, no softlocks, no black display on Pause/results/death, S2 save bytes unchanged, Classic and nonchallenge Enhanced game exact controls. One final private Windows build plus full source, visual comparisons, H: recovery, SHA receipt and one Chief play check. Do not require full original Sonic/Tails campaigns.

## Decisions to settle as part of sprint design (not blockers to conceptual feasibility)
- **How is Boss Rush entered?** Suggest a clear separate Enhanced challenge choice near Data Select or Enhanced title, not replacing five existing slots and not requiring a new save. Prefer an obvious selection rather than a hidden developer key.
- **Final boss route:** show Sonic full five-Emerald EEZ final phase in challenge by default, or respect fresh/zero Emerald route? Recommend deliberate full encounter for challenge value, with no persistence. Tails route unchanged unless Chief deliberately approves an alternate final phase.
- **Challenge record/reward:** visible clear/time score, replay, unlock policy and choice of ring count. Recommend minimal nonpersistent results first, and add per-character best-time records only if they can be isolated safely. This is not S2 campaign completion.

## Manager disposition
**S3 concept APPROVED IN PRINCIPLE (evaluation), not an automatic source-edit authorization or finalized task prompt**. A rich custom zone is more work than a boss menu but is aligned with Chief's desire for meaningful 1-sprint work. Keep [S2 accepted and six visual polish items](S2_CHIEF_ACCEPTANCE_20261105.md) independent and parked until later. S1/S2 final graphics defaults undecided. Do not commission a six-room build solely based on Manager's illustrative ring numbers before Chief approves the detailed gameplay contract.
