## AQZ — Aqua Planet 1 and 2

Status: **NOT TESTED by Research**. Chief reports consistent failures underwater in both acts; this card refines the sequence rather than disputing that report.

1. Select **Aqua Planet ACT-1**; reach an ordinary underwater floor. Move quickly right, use Chief's crouch/Space charge-and-release sequence, and observe actual movement against terrain, separately from sprite facing. Repeat left. Repeat in **ACT-2**.
2. Record whether Down was pressed while still moving, whether the opposite arrow had been used to brake, which arrow remained held during charging, and whether Down or Space was released first. Do not require frame-perfect imitation of the seeded Research trace.
3. As a focused additional attempt, brake toward the intended launch direction until nearly stopped, then hold Down, press Space, and release Down. A launch opposite the intended direction is the proven source mechanism's relevant symptom. A failure to reproduce this optional attempt is not proof that Chief's earlier report was incorrect.
4. Compare a same-direction launch from an ordinary stop and one dry launch. Record duration/distance of wrong-way movement and whether releasing/reapplying the arrow restores control; do not infer “indefinite” from a short observation.

Expected canonical release uses the retained input-facing direction and the normal dash launch. No speed clamp or changed underwater rules are expected. Terrain landmarks distinguish world movement from camera scrolling.

Status: **NOT TESTED by Research**; source-host trap reproduction **unresolved**.

1. For a direct route, set **CHAOS EMERALDS 2**, launch **Turquoise Hill ACT-1**, then pause → level select → **SS-3**. This establishes a known return origin (Turquoise Hill2); it is a shortcut fixture. Do not rerun the full campaign for this check.
2. Follow the ordinary stage path through the lower tube-mouth rooms. There are five regularly spaced lower monitors, plus one near the higher pool and one near the final section. Identify the affected one by room/landmark or count from the start; “top 10%” describes Sonic's depth into its upper side, not its stage location.
3. Try the reported upper-left approach during normal play without moving objects. Record whether Sonic is jumping/spinning or falling upright, whether the monitor breaks, whether he stands lower than its top, and whether Left/Right/jump or releasing input allows escape. If a particular approach cannot be reached naturally, mark it BLOCKED rather than using a teleport.
4. If continuing the successful route, **hold Up before the late branching junction**, rather than waiting for a fresh press exactly at the junction. This is the established route rule, not a proposed change.

Expected: contact may push sideways or support on top; attack posture and impact state determine breaking/bounce. A reproducible persistent stand inside the side needs its exact monitor and preceding inputs captured. A brief fractional visual overlap alone does not establish trapping.
