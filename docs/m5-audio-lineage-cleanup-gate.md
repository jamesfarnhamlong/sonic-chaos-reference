## What the upstream source actually shows

- Creator Joao Araya publicly introduced Open Sonic SMS on 6 July 2019 as the shared GameMaker/GML engine of the Sonic 1 and Sonic 2 SMS remakes: https://sonic-sms-remake.blogspot.com/2019/07/open-sonic-sms.html
- Public baseline `https://github.com/joaoaraya/prj-openSonicSMS` (`master` snapshot) includes `sound/audio/SFX_ring.mp3`, `SFX_sonic_spring.mp3`, `SND_power_invincibility.mp3`, and 33 published `sound/audio` files; `sound/SFX_ring.sound.gmx` declares a normal, streamed GameMaker MP3 audio asset. `objects/OBJ_monitor_invincibility.object.gmx` uses `audio_stop_all()` followed by `audio_play_sound(SND_power_invincibility,10,false)`. This demonstrates a viable sample-playback base, **not** an original Sonic Chaos Z80-driver/PSG emulator.
- Public Open Sonic SMS is an engine baseline, not necessarily the entire audio/content code from every shipped later Sonic 1/2 remake. We have not verified the complete shipped remakes' internal audio architecture. Do not claim that the upstream audio failed a controlled Sonic Chaos A/B test; no such test was found in current evidence.
- For a sample-driven remake, GameMaker built-in sounds are a legitimate simpler solution. Our original-SMS PSG/register-write, priority/steal/release/fade and exact continuous-output fidelity objective required additional work; its added complexity was a **design choice**, not evidence that Araya's base was bad. The Master System Sonic Chaos sound programme bank is game-specific.
- We must distinguish **gameplay engine** (keep Open Sonic SMS-derived mechanics/resources where still used) from **audio implementations**. No claim that we built three entire gameplay engines.
